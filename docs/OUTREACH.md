# Outreach drafts

Three short messages to send before the Monday 26 Oct 23:59 deadline, and what to say if someone replies. One real reply from a producer responsibility organisation (PRO), a buy-back centre or a reclaimer organisation is the cheapest way to raise the Sustainability and Viability scores, because the judges ask whether the numbers have been tested outside the team.

**Rules for every message**

- Say what exists today and what does not. We have a live app and one recorded test sale on a demo account. We do not have real reclaimers, real payouts or a signed partner. Do not claim otherwise.
- The R50 per verified tonne and the 2% buyer fee are our assumptions to test. Say "we would like to test".
- Never put the demo password in a message. Send the live link, and give the login on request or on a call.
- Fill in the names in square brackets. Contact details are not in this repo: use each organisation's own website or an introduction through Sonke mentors.

## 1. A PRO (Petco, Polyco, MetPac, Fibre Circle or the PRO Alliance)

**Subject:** A reclaimer-side app that records weigh-ins, for EPR proof

> Hi [name],
>
> I am [your name] from Team JBB, building ComeCollectZA for the FNB App of the Year Hackathon. It is a free web app for waste reclaimers: it shows which buy-back centre pays most today, and records every weighed sale so a reclaimer can prove their income and the producer service fee they are owed.
>
> We think this could also give you something you need: a verified record of where each tonne came from, with the material, centre and time, taken at the weigh-in. We would like to test whether a PRO would pay about R50 for each verified tonne reported (about 6.5% of Petco's R773 a tonne PET fee). That is an assumption, not an offer.
>
> It is live now at https://comecollectza.vercel.app and works offline on a phone. We have not yet run it with real reclaimers: we have one test sale on a demo account, and we say so.
>
> Could I have 15 minutes this week to ask whether this record would be useful to you, and what you would need to see before you trusted it? The demo login is available on request.
>
> Thank you,
> [your name], Team JBB

**If they reply with interest:** ask what format they receive collection data in today, who in the programme owns reclaimer registration, and whether a pilot at one centre is possible. Offer to share the aggregated, no-names PRO dashboard.

## 2. A buy-back centre (WhatsApp or a visit)

> Hi [name], I am [your name], a student building a free app for reclaimers in Soweto. It shows reclaimers what each centre pays, and gives them a QR code so you can record their weighed sale in seconds. It costs the centre nothing: you set your own price board and nothing changes about how you pay.
>
> Could I show you on my phone for 10 minutes, and could one or two of your regular reclaimers try it? It is at comecollectza.vercel.app

**What to ask them:** how do you record sales today (paper, a spreadsheet, nothing)? Which materials and prices matter most? Would a QR receipt save you time? Would you ever pay 2% on a sale if it saved you the book work (we do not charge anything now)?

## 3. A reclaimer organisation (African Reclaimers Organisation or SA Waste Pickers Association)

> Dear [name],
>
> I am [your name] from Team JBB. We have built a free web app, ComeCollectZA, that is made for reclaimers and never charges them: it shows which buy-back centre pays the most today, scans a load on the phone to name the material (without using data after the first download), and keeps a record of each sale and the service fee owed.
>
> Before we go further we want to hear from reclaimers, not guess. Could 3 to 5 of your members try it for 10 minutes and tell us what is wrong? We will change the app around what they say, and we will not collect any data beyond what the app needs to work.
>
> It is live at https://comecollectza.vercel.app. I can bring the phones and the data bundles for the test.
>
> Kind regards,
> [your name], Team JBB

**If they reply:** agree who tests, where and when, in what language, and how members are thanked for their time. Use [REAL_USER_TEST.md](REAL_USER_TEST.md).
