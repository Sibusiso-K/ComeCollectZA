# 20-second pitch video: script

Sonke's brief: a 20-second HustleWindow video that sells the app, under 30 MB, uploaded by Saturday 24 Oct at 13:30.

The video `ComeCollectZA-pitch-music.mp4` (20.0 s, 1920 x 1080, 10.9 MB) has the visuals and music. Record your voice over it with the lines below. Each line starts when its scene appears.

| Time | On screen | Say this |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0 to 3.4 s | **The problem.** Landfill footage. "Reclaimers collect our recycling by hand." | "Reclaimers recycle by hand. Most never know what a load is worth." |
| 3.4 to 6.3 s | **The problem.** A reclaimer carrying a load. "Prices are hidden. Pay is a guess." | "Prices are hidden, so pay is a guess." |
| 6.3 to 9.2 s | **The solution.** The scanner names the material. | "ComeCollectZA fixes that. Snap a photo, and it names the material." |
| 9.2 to 12.1 s | The best price at each centre. | "Then it shows the best price nearby." |
| 12.1 to 15.0 s | My money: the sale, payouts, SAWPRS. | "Sell, get paid, and every kilogram is on record." |
| 15.0 to 17.4 s | Old electronics: the scanner and an e-waste pile. | "Old phones and fridges too." |
| 17.4 to 20.0 s | Logo, "Fair pay for every kilogram", the link. | "ComeCollectZA. Fair pay for every kilogram." |

About 58 words. Speak slightly faster than normal, and pause for half a beat at 6.3 s, where the music lifts into the solution.

## Recording tips

- Play the video with headphones on, so the music does not get into your microphone, and read along.
- Quiet room, phone or microphone a hand's width from your mouth.
- Do three takes and keep the best one.
- If Sonke wants your face on screen, record a selfie video as you read. It can go in a corner of this video.
