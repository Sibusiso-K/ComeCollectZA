# Phone test

Steps to check the app on a real phone. Write down what you see, and send photos of anything wrong. Takes about 15 minutes.

**Address:** https://comecollectza.vercel.app/reclaimer

## Before you start

- Use a good signal or Wi-Fi for the first scan. It downloads about 23 MB once.
- Allow the camera when the browser asks.
- Android: use Chrome. iPhone: use Safari.

## 1. Open and install

| Step | You should see |
|---|---|
| Open the address above | The reclaimer home screen with a "Live prices" notice and prices for cans, PET and cardboard |
| Android: browser menu, then Install app. iPhone: Share, then Add to Home Screen | An icon with a green tile and "CC" on your home screen |
| Open it from the home screen | The app opens without the browser bar |
| Switch on flight mode, then open the app again | Pages you already visited still open. Others say you are offline |

## 2. Scan a load

Gather a few real items: a drink can, a plastic bottle, a piece of cardboard, a sheet of paper, a glass bottle. A pile is better than one item.

| Step | You should see |
|---|---|
| Tap Sell, then Open camera | The rear camera opens. The button says "Getting the scanner ready" with a percentage, then "Scan this load" |
| Point at a can or two and tap Scan this load | A suggestion such as "Looks like Metal cans", with a percentage and a time in milliseconds |
| Tap "Yes, Cans" | The Cans tile is selected and the board price shows |
| Scan a plastic bottle | "Plastic", with two buttons: PET bottles and HDPE |
| Scan something odd, such as your hand | "Not sure what this is", and a note to pick the material yourself |
| Tap "Take or choose a photo" and pick a picture from your gallery | The same kind of suggestion |
| Enter a weight on the keypad | The estimate changes with the price |

Record, for each scan: what it was, what the app said, how sure it was, and whether the first suggestion was right. If it is wrong often, note the light and the angle.

## 3. Scan a reclaimer code

1. On your phone, open Sell, pick a material, enter a weight, and tap Show my code. A QR code appears.
2. On a second phone or a laptop, open https://comecollectza.vercel.app/centre and tap Scan the code.
3. Point it at the first phone's QR code. You should see `ccza_r_7Q2KX9` appear in the Reclaimer code field.

## 4. Look and feel

- Is the text readable in bright sunlight? Are the buttons easy to hit with a thumb?
- Does anything scroll sideways or get hidden behind the bottom tabs?
- Does it work in dark mode and light mode?

## Known limits

- The scanner is trained on clean photos. Real piles may score lower. It always asks you to confirm.
- Finding the reclaimer from the scanned code is not connected yet.
- Prices, centres and the reclaimer's name are demo data. The notices say so.
